01 / What is Sporting Intelligence?

Sporting Intelligence is a connected intelligence and due-diligence workspace for the business of sport. It brings counterparties, ownership, capital, screening, evidence and decisions into one operating picture.

02 / Why Sporting Intelligence?

The rules are changing.
The scrutiny is real.

Football is increasingly expected to understand who it is doing business with, who ultimately owns or controls an entity, where money comes from and whether the evidence stands up to scrutiny.

FIFA Regulations on the Status and Transfer of Players / intermediary framework

Integrity, transparency and oversight expectations across football relationships. Applicability depends on the activity and current regulatory framework.

UK Economic Crime and Corporate Transparency Act 2023

Companies House reforms strengthen identity, filing and beneficial ownership information, while improving the reliability of the UK company register.

UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017

Relevant regulated firms must apply risk-based customer due diligence, understand beneficial ownership and scrutinise source of funds where required.

FATF standards and risk-based approach

International benchmarks for identifying beneficial ownership, assessing risk and applying proportionate controls. Not every football organisation is directly in scope.

// Regulatory obligations vary by jurisdiction, entity type and regulated activity.

03 / What problem are we solving?

The information exists.
The joined-up picture doesn't.

A club may be known by one name, owned through another company and funded by a layered group of investors. Evidence is scattered across registries, filings, screening providers, news, documents and conversations.

PROBLEM / 01

Fragmented identities

Club, company, holding company, owner and investor are different entity types. Disconnected records create blind spots and false matches.

PROBLEM / 02

Opaque capital

Transaction headlines rarely explain the full funding chain, investor vehicle, debt, equity commitments or ultimate source of funds.

PROBLEM / 03

Evidence without workflow

Analysts need traceable facts, reported claims, hypotheses, open questions, review decisions and requests — not another disconnected search result.

goal: reduce uncertainty before it becomes transaction risk.
